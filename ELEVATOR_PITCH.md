## About the Project

What inspired us to build Burrowly was seeing how disconnected people can feel in their own neighborhoods, especially during tough times. We noticed that vulnerable residents—elderly folks, people with disabilities, newcomers who don't speak the language—often struggle to get help when they need it most, while at the same time there are neighbors nearby who want to help but don't know how. During emergencies, official services get overwhelmed, but research shows that **80-90% of life-saving rescues are done by neighbors**. We wanted to bridge that gap and make it easier for communities to take care of their own.

Through building this project, we learned so much about creating accessible technology that actually works for everyone. We discovered how to integrate multiple AWS services together, how to design for accessibility from the ground up (not as an afterthought), and how AI can be used meaningfully to break down real barriers like language and prioritization. We built Burrowly using **React** for the frontend, **AWS Lambda** and **API Gateway** for the backend, **DynamoDB** for data storage, and integrated AWS AI services like **Comprehend** and **Translate**. We used **Firebase** for authentication and **Mapbox** for the interactive maps. Everything was built on free-tier services to prove that powerful civic technology can be accessible and sustainable.

The biggest challenges we faced were making sure the platform was truly accessible—not just compliant, but actually usable by people with different needs. We had to think about screen readers, color blindness, motor impairments, and cognitive differences. Another challenge was designing the AI integration to be helpful without being intrusive, and making sure the translation and categorization actually worked in real-world scenarios. We also struggled with balancing features—wanting to build everything but needing to focus on what would actually make a difference. In the end, we created something that we're genuinely proud of, not just because it works, but because it could actually help real people in real communities.

## Elevator Pitch (30 seconds)

Burrowly is basically a way for neighbors to help each other out when someone needs a hand. Imagine your elderly neighbor needs groceries during a snowstorm, or someone in a wheelchair can't get up a blocked ramp—instead of waiting for city services or struggling alone, they can post a quick request and nearby volunteers get notified instantly. We use AI to translate requests so language isn't a barrier, and to prioritize urgent needs so the most critical situations get help first. It's like having a neighborhood support network that's always there, powered by technology that actually makes sense. We built this because we believe communities are stronger when people look out for each other, and sometimes you just need a way to connect the person who needs help with the person who can give it.

## Extended Pitch (60 seconds)

You know how sometimes you see someone struggling and wish you could help, but you don't know how? Or maybe you're the one who needs help—maybe you're elderly and can't shovel your walk, or you use a wheelchair and a ramp is blocked, or you just moved here and don't speak the language well. That's where Burrowly comes in.

It's pretty simple: someone posts what they need, and people nearby who can help get a notification. The AI figures out how urgent it is and matches it with volunteers who have the right skills. If someone posts in Spanish, volunteers see it in English automatically. If someone can't see well, the app adjusts. It's designed so everyone can use it, because everyone should be able to ask for help when they need it.

We built this because we saw that during emergencies, official services get overwhelmed, but neighbors are always there. We wanted to make it easier for that natural community support to happen. Plus, we built it all on free tools so any city or neighborhood could actually use it without breaking the bank. At the end of the day, it's about making sure nobody has to face tough situations alone.
